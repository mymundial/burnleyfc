# Sudden death by mode

- Competition mode: both teams take exactly five penalties; the match finishes even if level. No sixth penalty or sudden-death marker is offered. The competition leaderboard continues to use only the first five player shots for a maximum of 1,000 points.
- Practice mode: retains sudden death when a winner is required and the match is level after five penalties each. Both teams take one extra penalty per sudden-death round until there is a winner.
- T&Cs section 6 now explicitly distinguishes the five-penalty competition limit from practice mode.
- Match completion determination is shared across live shots and restored snapshots so both behave consistently.
- No other gameplay or presentation changes are intended.
