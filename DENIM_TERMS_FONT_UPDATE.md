# Denim WD — Terms and Conditions typography pass

Scope: the scrollable T&C text only. No terms wording, gameplay, registration,
modal dimensions, buttons, leaderboard, or other screen styling is changed.

- Numbered section headings: Denim WD **Light (300)**, **18px**, uppercase,
  normal (upright) style, **1.35** line height.
- Paragraphs and lists: Denim WD **Regular (400)**, **14px**, **1.6** line height.
- Gaps: **12px** between heading and first paragraph, **10px** between distinct
  paragraphs/lists, **24px** between numbered sections.
- Modal title and the BACK TO ENTRY button retain National 2 styling.

## Installing the fonts

Place both appropriately licensed files at:

- `public/fonts/denim/DenimWD-Light.otf`
- `public/fonts/denim/DenimWD-Regular.otf`

The supplied trial fonts are licensed for local evaluation, **not public
production use**, and are not included in these ZIPs. For a local preview you
may rename copies of `DenimWD-TRIAL-Light.otf` and
`DenimWD-TRIAL-Regular.otf` to those filenames. Use licensed fonts before
public deployment.
